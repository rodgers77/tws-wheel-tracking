TWS WHEEL TRACKING - how to put it on a phone
=============================================
App address: https://rodgers77.github.io/tws-wheel-tracking/
Office screen: https://rodgers77.github.io/tws-wheel-tracking/office.html
Note: this is a public web page, so anyone with the link can open it.

INSTALL ON ANDROID (Chrome)
1. Open Chrome and go to the app address above.
2. Tap the 3 dots (top right) > Install app (or Add to Home screen).
3. Tap Install / Add. A "TWS Wheels" icon appears on the home screen.
4. Always open the app from that icon.

INSTALL ON iPHONE (Safari - must be Safari, not Chrome)
1. Open Safari and go to the app address above.
2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
4. Always open the app from the "TWS Wheels" icon.

PICK THE STATION FOR THIS PHONE
1. Open the app and scroll to the bottom.
2. Choose the station from the dropdown (e.g. "4 Strip-down", "8 Oven", "13 Fit to car").
3. The phone remembers that station every time the app is opened, so it stays set
   for that bench. Only change it if the phone moves to a different station.
   (There is no password lock yet - ask staff not to change the dropdown.)

THE OFFICE
The office uses office.html (the Office screen link above), or any phone set to
station "3 Office / Hold / Status". It shows where every job is, all scans,
wrong-scan alerts, completed-job alerts, Hold/Release, and Export (CSV/JSON).

USING IT
Each tick and "Done" is saved with the person's name and the time. Scans in the
wrong order are rejected and flagged to the office screen.

UPDATES
When the app is changed, phones pick up the new version next time they open it
while online. If it looks out of date, close the app fully and open it again.

*** WARNING - EACH PHONE KEEPS ITS OWN DATA FOR NOW ***
Scans are saved only on the phone they were made on. The office screen only
shows scans made on THAT phone - it cannot see what other phones have scanned yet.
Sharing between phones needs a small online service setting up (not done yet).
Until then, use Export on each phone to collect the records. Clearing the browser
data or deleting the app will wipe that phone's records, so export them first.
